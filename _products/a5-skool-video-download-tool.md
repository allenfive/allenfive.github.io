## Overview

The Skool Video Download Tool is a Chrome extension that allows you to download course videos from Skool platforms and save them as MP4 files on your local device. Perfect for offline viewing and content backup.

## Key Features

- **One-click download**: Easily download videos with a single click
- **MP4 format**: Videos are automatically converted and saved as MP4 files
- **Local storage**: All downloads are saved directly to your device
- **Simple interface**: Clean, intuitive Chrome extension interface
- **Fast processing**: Efficient video processing and conversion

## How It Works

1. Install the Skool Video Download Tool extension from the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/search/Skool%20Video%20Download%20Tools%20-%20by%20A5)
2. Navigate to any Skool course page with video content
3. Click the extension and choose the download option
4. The video is processed and downloaded as an MP4 file to your default download folder
5. Enjoy your videos offline anytime
